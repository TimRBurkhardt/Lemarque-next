# LEMARQUE — Design Context

## Design Context

### Users
Buyers and collectors of handmade, one-of-one designer fashion. The creator positions the pieces from €6,000 upward (24 October 2026 brief). Mobile discovery and deliberate desktop browsing. The job is to discover a collection, inspect the actual piece and contact the house. Prices remain upon request; there is no checkout.

### Brand Personality
**Dark, exacting, rare.** Editorial confidence, sculptural clothing and tangible construction. An uncompromising designer house. Original LEMARQUE wordmark and crest are retained.

### Design Principles
1. Give a piece room to command attention before introducing the next.
2. Use studio photographs for product truth; campaign imagery establishes mood.
3. Guide visitors from the collection to the piece to a personal inquiry.
4. Keep all published pieces reachable through explicit controls, including on mobile.
5. Use motion to clarify interaction and rhythm; keep the clothes visible and the browser in the visitor's control.

### Quality Bar
Production-quality brand experience. See DESIGN-CONCEPT.md for the rationale and page sequence. Existing shop URLs and product hashes must continue to work.
