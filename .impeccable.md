# LEMARQUE — Design Context

## Design Context

### Users
Buyers and collectors of handmade, one-of-one designer fashion. The creator positions the pieces from €6,000 upward (24 October 2026 brief). Mobile discovery and deliberate desktop browsing. The job is to discover a collection, inspect the actual piece and contact the house. Prices remain upon request; there is no checkout.

### Brand Personality
**Dark, exacting, rare.** Editorial confidence, sculptural clothing and tangible construction. An uncompromising designer house. Original LEMARQUE wordmark and crest are retained.

### Aesthetic Direction — MALUM, 13 November 2026
- The creator approved the Bécane-inspired spatial collection overview as the basis for MALUM, then asked for an own-brand refinement using Impeccable.
- MALUM uses a light neutral surface, small static garment-only packshots in a wide desktop lineup and a mobile circle. The creator subsequently approved this direction for product detail pages as well. Other collection pages and the homepage keep their existing design.
- Header: small original crest centered, no LEMARQUE wordmark; compact menu at the left and a collection-return control at the right. The shared menu opens as a broad light-dismiss panel with large typographic links on the left and a garment-led current-collection preview on the right; mobile stacks the same navigation. No numbered links, cards or blur.
- The creator explicitly requested a slightly larger garment on hover and an appearing image-switching UI using Huashu Motion. The refined desktop preview scales to 1.55 over 480 ms; neighboring images shift 40 px away. Tablet uses 1.40 / 24 px; touch selection uses 1.30. Hovering a thumbnail in the desktop preview switches the image immediately; touch retains tap selection. Pointer exit closes the image controls even after a thumbnail click. Keyboard focus opens controls and Escape dismisses them. Reduced Motion disables transforms and transitions.
- The default five images remain garment-only. Optional photo selections use existing original product photography, including studio views clearly labeled as such. Image selection also works in the large detail view on touch devices.
- Title sits at the upper right; the large item view places the garment left and information, image controls and navigation right. Keep “Back to collection.” On 14 November the creator requested further simplification: the overview title is a small 22 px MALUM at the upper right (18 px mobile), with no Collection subtitle. Remove the bottom Manufactured attire signature. Replace the large statement heading and paragraph with one compact line, followed by the existing footer links. Detail-view product headings and navigation remain legible. A subsequent graphic-design pass aligns the collection on a shared five-column desktop grid: header, image centers, left-aligned title in the final column, Explore pieces and footer links share axes and fluid outer margins. Mobile uses two text columns around the retained garment circle. Hover captions pair a quiet piece number with a sentence-case name.
- Entrance revised after the creator rejected the automatic photo changes as strange: one coherent right-to-left reveal, 840 ms ease-out per garment with 90 ms stagger (1.2 s total), 12 px settling distance, stable original silhouettes. No automatic photo changes or scale pulses. Manual hover photo transitions shortened to 180 ms. Interaction, scrolling, leaving the tab and Reduced Motion cancel/skip the sequence. Timing and verification: design/malum-entrance-v24.json.
- Mobile uses two taps, explicitly confirmed by the creator: first selects and retains an image picker below the circular lineup; second opens the large collection view. A close control dismisses the selection. The product URL is opened only through “View piece.”
- Preserve the original MALUM campaign experience at `/collection/malum/classic`, using the original `CollectionDetailClient` and campaign hero. The current minimal design remains at `/collection/malum`.
- Product detail pages now use the same light surface and shared centered-crest header. Large original photographs, mouse-hover thumbnail selection and tap/arrow/swipe navigation, title and inquiry at the right, and original related-piece routes. Preserve real product descriptions and price upon request.
- Latest implementation and verification: design/product-design-v23.json; collection interaction checks: design/malum-design-pass-v22.json. Earlier local versions remain archived.

### Design Principles
1. Give a piece room to command attention before introducing the next.
2. Use studio photographs for product truth; campaign imagery establishes mood.
3. Guide visitors from the collection to the piece to a personal inquiry.
4. Keep all published pieces reachable through explicit controls, including on mobile.
5. Use motion to clarify interaction and rhythm; keep the clothes visible and the browser in the visitor's control.

### Quality Bar
Production-quality brand experience. See DESIGN-CONCEPT.md for the rationale and page sequence. Existing shop URLs and product hashes must continue to work.
